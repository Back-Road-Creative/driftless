How to read this map

Every process, technique and artifact this catalog knows, with every tie between them drawn as a line. Three kinds, one shape each: {{ kind_marks.process }} a process — a step the method names, drawn as a filled square, tinted by its knowledge area; {{ kind_marks.technique }} a technique — a way of doing one, drawn as a filled dot; {{ kind_marks.artifact }} an artifact — a document or record a process reads or produces, drawn as a filled diamond. Every shape links to its own page; a technique or artifact's own name shows on hover, on keyboard focus, or once a filter narrows the map to a handful of nodes. A shape's summary and its ties show while you hover it or tab to it; press Space on a shape you have tabbed to to keep them up.
